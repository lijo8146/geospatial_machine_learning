# Data boundaries

`generated/`: reproducible synthetic practice products from the notebooks, never observations.
`real/`: your downloaded observations, labeled points, provenance and real-model results.
`models/`: external checkpoints. All three are ignored by Git.

`labels.example.csv` is a header-only schema, not a training dataset. No external datasets or pretrained weights are bundled. The synthetic data is generated by `course.py` under this repository's license. Keep third-party source terms and attribution with real data. Do not commit accounts, credentials or large downloads.
